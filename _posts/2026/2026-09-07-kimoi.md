우리는 사람의 욕망을 처벌하면 안 된다.
우리는 사람을 좋아하지 않을 수 있다. 혐오할 수도 있다. (내 개인적인 의견으로, 혐오는 어느 정도 선호의 부재이다)

### Introduction

This is something I have been thinking about a lot lately, and in my head it felt utterly unpleasant, yet I simply could not deny it. **I am strongly opposed to acting on pedophilia, and I think it is morally/legally wrong.** This is all because the company is near Akihabara. I have an inconvenient habit of thinking about strange things when I see them.

We must not punish people's desires.
We may not like people. We may even hate them. (In my personal opinion, hatred is, to some extent, the absence of preference)
Just as it is impossible to ask people not to like tall, handsome men and please like short men for a change (I am short, so that contributes a little more to my welfare), we dislike, to some degree, appearances and personalities that are not preferred. Rather than dislike them, we are not favorably disposed toward them.

However, there is a slight difference between a preference being expressed as a signal of liking and a non-preference being expressed as a signal of hatred. Modern people in the 21st century do not discriminate against others. At least most of the human beings envisioned by modern society probably do not live consciously hating those they “treat as human beings.”

I can make exactly the same argument regarding incest (without coercion arising from hierarchy) and pedophilia (not acted out on others).
